Manifesto

Our Sustainable Heritage & Craft | Zero-Waste Titanium Eyewear Philosophy

Founded in Kyoto and refined across the Alpine workshops of Cadore. Proving that minimalist perfection and circular metallurgy co-exist without compromise.

01 • Metallurgy

Reclaimed Aerospace Grade 5 Titanium

We upcycle certified Grade 5 aerospace titanium remnants from precision manufacturing in Nagoya, Japan.

Billets are re-melted under an inert argon shroud, cutting carbon emissions by 84% while preserving aerospace tensile strength and total biocompatibility.

View Titanium Frames
02 • Chemistry

Zero-Waste Bio-Acetate Philosophy

Our bio-acetate editions utilize Mazzucchelli M49, formulated from certified European cotton seeds and FSC wood pulp.

Bonded strictly without petroleum-derived phthalates, each frame biodegrades completely in industrial compost within 115 days without chemical residues.

View Bio-Acetate Frames
03 • Artisanship

The Master Artisans of Sabae & Cadore

In Sabae, Fukui Prefecture, our craftsmen perform over 180 hands-on operations per frame to maintain sub-millimeter tolerances.

Every frame is tumbled for 72 continuous hours in rotating barrels with birch granules and crushed walnut shells, achieving an enduring velvet satin touch.

Production

From Aerospace Remnant to Optical Heirloom

A transparent chronicle of the 4 key stages behind every serialized frame.

01

Reclamation & Ingot Cast

Aerospace scrap is spectroscopically sorted and remelted under inert argon with zero primary ore mining.

02

5-Axis Cold Milling

Diamond tooling mills bridge contours and screwless flex hinge sockets to sub-20 micron tolerances.

03

Organic Walnut Tumbling

Three-stage gentle agitation in birch and crushed walnut granules produces a soft satin luster.

04

Zeiss Optical Calibration

Zeiss mineral lenses are acoustically sealed into the chassis for true optical clarity.
